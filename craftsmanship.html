Our Method

Bespoke, Conscious & Handcrafted

Each tablet is an individual piece of sculpture. We melt premium eco-friendly soy wax, carefully blend therapeutic-grade scents at optimum temperatures, and hand-arrange botanicals in dynamic configurations.

Natural Preservation

Naturally releases high-clarity scent for up to 6 months without synthetic combustion.

Local Floral Scavenging

Every floral stem is handpicked and solar-dehydrated locally to retain organic color.
