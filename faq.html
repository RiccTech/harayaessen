Common Curiosities

Wax sachets are designed to scent small enclosed spaces. Hang them in closets on hangers, place them inside dresser drawers, shoe cabinets, or leave them on a decorative dish in your powder room. Avoid leaving them in direct contact with fabrics to prevent potential oil transfer.
Typically, our wax sachets continue to throw off scent for 4 to 6 months depending on the ventilation level of the space. Once you feel the fragrance has decreased, you can actually break the tablet into pieces and melt them in a wax burner to release the final residual essential oils!
Natural soy wax has a melting temperature of roughly 120°F (50°C). While they are highly resilient in typical interior settings, we do not recommend hanging them in cars during hot summers or placing them directly over thermal heating vents.
